Zero-Latency Intelligence

Six brains. One grounded learner experience.

Every session routes through specialized intelligence modules, then returns a single coherent response grounded in retrieved evidence and learner history — with no round-trip drag.

BRAIN 01 · ORCH

Orchestrator

Classifies intent, dispatches specialist brains, and assembles one aligned response.

BRAIN 02 · TEACH

Teaching Brain

Builds native explanations and adapts teaching approach to the learner's state.

BRAIN 03 · MEM

Memory Brain

Maintains persistent learner context, vocabulary profile, and struggle patterns.

BRAIN 04 · CULT

Cultural Brain

Retrieves locally effective examples and improves from actual learning outcomes.

BRAIN 05 · EVAL

Evaluator Brain

Scores mastery against rubric evidence instead of simple completion behavior.

BRAIN 06 · CURR

Curriculum Brain

Turns briefs into skill clusters, nodes, prerequisites, and mastery thresholds.
